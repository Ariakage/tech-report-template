\hcsecbi{结论与后续方向}{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\hcbipair{%
本文提出了面向异步生理遥测与纵向临床监测的校准优先融合框架 \textbf{AuroraCard}。通过联合连续时间隐状态衰减、可靠度加权融合与共形温度路由，系统兼具高判别精度、有限样本不确定性覆盖保证与低于 15\,ms 的边缘推理时延。%
}{%
We introduced \textbf{AuroraCard}, a calibration-first multimodal fusion framework tailored to asynchronous physiological telemetry and longitudinal clinical surveillance. Combining continuous-time latent decay with reliability-weighted fusion and conformal temperature routing yields strong discrimination, finite-sample coverage guarantees, and sub-15\,ms edge latency.%
}

\begin{hccaveat}[\hcbisep{临床部署边界提示}{Clinical Deployment Caveat}]
  \hcbipair{%
    尽管 VitalBench-26 覆盖四家地理分布各异的医疗中心，前瞻性床旁上线仍需开展院区阈值治理、人工复核分级报警与持续亚组公平性审计~\cite{rajpurkar2022ai}。全部回顾性队列均经机构伦理豁免并去标识化。%
  }{%
    While VitalBench-26 spans four medical centers, prospective bedside deployment still requires site-specific threshold governance, human-in-the-loop alert escalation policies, and continuous subgroup fairness auditing~\cite{rajpurkar2022ai}. All retrospective cohorts were de-identified under institutional ethics waivers.%
  }
\end{hccaveat}

\hcparbi{后续研究方向}{Future Directions}
\hcbipair{%
后续工作包括通过检索增强适配器融合结构化病程文本、在隐私保护医疗联盟内开展联邦共形校准，以及针对可穿戴分诊设备的流式硬件量化。%
}{%
Promising extensions include adding structured clinician progress notes via retrieval-augmented adapters, federated conformal calibration across privacy-preserving hospital consortia, and streaming hardware quantization for wearable triage monitors.%
}
